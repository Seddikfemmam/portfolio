Research Master’s Graduation Project

In progress

A Multimodal Deep Learning Framework for Automatic Salient Event Detection in Recorded Sessions

Master’s thesis — Master 2 RTIC (Networks and Information & Communication Technologies), final year. Preceded by a Licence degree in Computer Science.

This research designs and develops a multimodal deep learning framework for the automatic detection of salient events in recorded sessions — online lectures, professional meetings, webinars and conferences. The approach leverages several sources of information at once (audiovisual content, speech transcripts and semantic cues) to identify the most relevant and informative segments of a session.

Features extracted from each modality are fused inside a multimodal deep learning architecture that detects significant events such as key explanations, demonstrations, decision-making moments and conclusions.

Natural Language Processing

Speech transcripts and semantic cues used to locate meaningful discourse.

Computer Vision

Visual features from slides, demonstrations and on-screen activity.

Audio Analysis

Acoustic patterns and speaker dynamics signalling important moments.
  • Cut down the time needed to review long recordings by giving rapid access to essential information.
  • Support automatic video summarization of recorded sessions.
  • Enable intelligent indexing of multimedia content.
  • Build towards smart tools for learning assistance and knowledge management.
